# Theatres

Interactive 3D exploration of theatre spaces at the intersection of software, spatial visualization and theatre research.

The project explores how digital 3D environments can be used to represent, inspect and study theatrical space.

## Concept

Theatre can be understood as a spatial system in which bodies, architecture, scenic elements, audience, light and movement establish relationships. Theatres experiments with representing those relationships through an interactive digital environment.

## Technical direction

- 3D scene construction and visualization.
- Interactive spatial interfaces.
- Theatre architecture and stage configurations.
- Digital representation of scenic space.
- JavaScript-based creative technology.
- Research and creative practice through software.
